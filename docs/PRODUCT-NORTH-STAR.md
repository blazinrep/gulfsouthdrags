# GulfSouthDrags Product North Star

_Last updated: 2026-09-14_

## The core idea

**Know before you tow.**

GulfSouthDrags is not trying to become another generic race-track directory. It is a **regional racer-intelligence product** for grassroots drag racing in Mississippi, Louisiana, and Alabama.

The directory is the surface. **Certainty is the product.**

A racer should be able to answer, before hooking up the trailer:

- Is this track actually operating?
- Is racing actually happening this weekend?
- When do gates and tech open?
- What classes, payout, entry fees, and race format are confirmed?
- Has anything changed since the flyer or Facebook post I saw?
- Can I park a trailer or RV overnight?
- Is the information current enough to trust?

## Who the product is for

Primary user: the racer or race family deciding **where to tow and whether the trip is worth making**.

Secondary users: track owners and operators, race promoters, racing series, performance shops and regional sponsors, and eventually other businesses that could use TrackWatch.

## Why GulfSouthDrags can matter

Grassroots racing information is fragmented across Facebook posts, flyers, track websites, series pages, old directory listings, and word of mouth. The failure is usually not lack of information; it is lack of **current, reconciled, trustworthy information**.

GulfSouthDrags should win by being narrower and deeper than national directories: drag racing only, Gulf South regional focus, explicit operating status, visible last-checked dates, useful racer logistics, event and series relationships, human review of important changes, and no pretending that unknown information is known.

## The trust system

Possible states:

- **Owner verified** — a track owner or promoter directly confirmed the information.
- **GulfSouthDrags checked** — the team independently checked a public source.
- **TrackWatch detected** — TrackWatch found a meaningful source change that requires review.
- **Unconfirmed** — information is missing, conflicting, or stale enough that we should not claim certainty.

The labels can evolve, but the principle should not.

## The independence rule

**Sponsors can buy exposure. Nobody can buy the truth.**

Commercial placement and factual verification must remain separate. No sponsor, track, promoter, or advertiser can purchase a false open/closed status, a stronger verification label than the evidence supports, removal of a legitimate warning, or altered factual race information.

## The product wedge

National sites can cover more tracks. GulfSouthDrags should know the Gulf South better.

The useful question is not simply:

> Where are the drag strips?

It is:

> Where can I actually race this weekend, and what do I need to know before I tow there?

That is the wedge.

## Important product directions

### Racer logistics

Prioritize gate time, tech time, race start time, classes, payout, entry and spectator price, race surface and distance, sanctioning body, overnight trailer/RV parking, hookups, fuel/race-fuel availability, weather-sensitive status when appropriate, and official-source links.

### State and regional hubs

State hubs such as `/drag-strips/mississippi/` should capture broad search intent while giving racers an immediate operating-status overview.

Do not damage track pages already earning search visibility merely to chase new keywords. Preserve URLs and strengthen them carefully.

### Weekend Tow Report

A future email product should not be a generic newsletter.

**THE WEEKEND TOW REPORT**

A short Gulf South briefing showing where racing is actually happening that weekend, important changes, major events, and unresolved items worth checking before leaving home.

### Track owner / promoter update path

Track owners and promoters should eventually have an obvious way to verify or correct their information. The form should collect useful race-day intelligence, not just a name and phone number.

### TrackWatch

TrackWatch is strategically important because it can make GulfSouthDrags harder to copy.

Intended model:

public source watchers -> meaningful-change detection -> comparison against current data -> private review queue -> human approval -> proposed site update

TrackWatch should assist judgment, not silently rewrite public facts.

## What GulfSouthDrags is not

Avoid drifting into a generic motorsports news site, a nationwide directory before the regional product is strong, low-value SEO pages created only for keywords, programmatic-ad clutter that reduces trust, a social feed that merely republishes flyers, or invented operational details presented as facts.

## Near-term success criteria

1. Become genuinely useful to Gulf South racers.
2. Grow search association with local track and state-level drag-racing queries.
3. Use TrackWatch to improve freshness without sacrificing human review.
4. Earn the **first $100 of real revenue** from a product that fits the audience.
5. Learn what racers, promoters, and sponsors actually value before overbuilding paid features.

## Decision filter

Before adding a major feature, ask:

1. Does this help a racer decide whether, where, or when to tow?
2. Does it increase freshness or trustworthiness?
3. Does it make GulfSouthDrags meaningfully better than a static directory or Facebook search?
4. Can we maintain it honestly?
5. Does it strengthen a future revenue path without weakening trust?

If the answer is mostly no, it is probably a distraction.
