Gulfport venue change + what's next

Venue change confirmed: Great American 50Ks moves to Gulfport, plus Sept. 19-26 race intel.

Published 18 September 2026 · Checked 18 September 2026

Sponsors can buy exposure. Nobody can buy the truth.

!VENUE CHANGED
NOW AT GULFPORT DRAGWAY

The SDPC Raceshop Great American 50Ks has moved from Montgomery International Dragway to Gulfport Dragway in Gulfport, Mississippi.

  • Parking begins Monday, October 12.
  • Test & Tune runs Tuesday, October 13, 11 AM-2 PM.
  • Four straight $50,000-to-win races run October 14-17.
  • Regular gate entry is available without pre-registration.
