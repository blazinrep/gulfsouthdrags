Footbrake 150 is on — Saturday is stacked

Gulfport's $25K weekend is underway. I-22, Swamp Bottom and No Problem all run Saturday. One Friday cancellation, one deadline today.

Published 13 November 2026 · Checked 13 November 2026

!FOOTBRAKE 150
TWIN $25K MAINS AT GULFPORT, NOV 13–15

The 3rd Annual Forever Footbrake 150 is underway at Gulfport Dragway. Twin $25,000-to-win main events run Saturday and Sunday. Friday adds Test n Tune ($25), a $3,000-to-win Gamblers Race, and an $8,000-to-win No Guts, No Glory race. Gate entry $150 per day. Spectators $15 per day, $40 weekend pass, kids 8 and under free. Pre-registration has closed. Sellout status is unverified.

  • Twin $25,000-to-win mains Saturday and Sunday ($2,000 runner-up, $750 semi-finalists, round money from round 4).
  • Friday: Test n Tune $25; $3,000 Gamblers Race; $8,000 No Guts, No Glory race.
  • Gate entry $150 per day. Spectators $15 per day, $40 weekend pass, kids 8 and under free.
  • Pre-registration closed. Sellout status unverified — do not claim it is sold out.

SATURDAY IS THE DAY

The cold moved the weekend to Saturday

Friday night racing at I-22 is off — temperatures dropping into the low 30s. Swamp Bottom moved its Saturday start earlier for the cold: gates 11 AM, track hot noon (was gates 2 PM). Saturday is the day: I-22 gates open 9 AM, Swamp Bottom 11 AM, No Problem 3 PM. At I-22 the forecast is mid-50s and sunny.

  • I-22 Friday Nov 13 cancelled for cold. Saturday only.
  • Swamp Bottom Saturday start moved earlier: gates 11 AM, track hot noon.
  • Three tracks run Saturday: I-22 (9 AM), Swamp Bottom (11 AM), No Problem (3 PM).
