What drag strips are open in Alabama?

The Alabama tracks currently confirmed operating by Gulf South Drags are Holiday Raceway in Woodstock, Montgomery International Dragway in Montgomery, I-22 Motorsports Park in Eldridge, Alabama International Dragway in Steele, Baileyton Goodtime Dragstrip in Baileyton. Which Alabama drag strips are quarter-mile tracks?

Montgomery International Dragway in Montgomery, Alabama International Dragway in Steele are the quarter-mile tracks in Alabama. Holiday Raceway in Woodstock, I-22 Motorsports Park in Eldridge, Baileyton Goodtime Dragstrip in Baileyton run eighth-mile.

Is Atmore Dragstrip in Atmore still open?

Atmore Dragstrip in Atmore, Alabama is permanently closed. Google lists the business as permanently closed, though it still shows a 4.5 rating from 355 reviews from when it was operating.
