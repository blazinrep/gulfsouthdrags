Common questions

What drag strips are open in Louisiana?

The Louisiana tracks currently confirmed operating by Gulf South Drags are No Problem Raceway Park in Belle Rose, Twin City Raceway in Monroe. The site last completed its current data check on 19 November 2026.

When is the next drag race in Louisiana?

Gamblers Race and Saturday Bracket Race — No Problem Raceway at No Problem Raceway Park, Friday 20 to Sunday 22 November 2026. See the event page for the full schedule.

Which Louisiana drag strips are quarter-mile tracks?

No Problem Raceway Park in Belle Rose is the quarter-mile track in Louisiana. Twin City Raceway in Monroe runs eighth-mile.

Is State Capitol Raceway in Port Allen still open?

State Capitol Raceway in Port Allen, Louisiana is closed for the 2026 season. The track announced at its January 2026 banquet that it had lost the lease on the top end of the strip.
