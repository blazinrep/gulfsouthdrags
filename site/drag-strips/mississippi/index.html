Common questions

What drag strips are open in Mississippi?

The Mississippi tracks currently confirmed operating by Gulf South Drags are Swamp Bottom Dragstrip in Columbia, Gulfport Dragway in Gulfport, Byram Dragway in Byram. The site last completed its current data check on 18 September 2026.

What is the closest operating drag strip to Hattiesburg, Mississippi?

Swamp Bottom Dragstrip in Columbia is the closest confirmed operating track in our current directory, about 34 straight-line miles from Hattiesburg.

Is Hub City Dragway in Hattiesburg still open?

No. Hub City Dragway is permanently closed and the land has been sold. Its old web presence is still online, which is why stale directories can be misleading.
