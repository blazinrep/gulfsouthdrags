3rd Annual Forever Footbrake 150

Gulfport Dragway — Gulfport, MS
Friday 13 to Sunday 15 November 2026

The 3rd Annual Forever Footbrake 150 runs November 13-15 at Gulfport Dragway with twin $25,000-to-win races.

Schedule

Friday 13 November
$3,000-to-win Gamblers Race and $8,000-to-win No Guts, No Glory race.
Saturday 14 - Sunday 15 November
Twin $25,000-to-win main events ($2,000 runner-up, $750 semi-finalists; round money from round 4 winners).

At the gate

Gate entry
$150 per day if the event is not sold out (BOGO and refund cutoff passed 29 October; confirm sellout status with the promoter)

What to expect

Twin $25,000-to-win main events with $2,000 runner-up and $750 semi-finalist money. Friday adds a $3,000 Gamblers Race and an $8,000 No Guts, No Glory race. The BOGO pre-registration offer and the refund cutoff both expired 29 October 2026; post-cutoff terms are $150-per-day gate entries if the event is not sold out. Sellout status is unverified: do not claim the event is sold out. Confirm current entry terms with the promoter before travelling.
