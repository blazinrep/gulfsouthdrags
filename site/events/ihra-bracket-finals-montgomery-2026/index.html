What to expect

This is the IHRA Southern Team Finals for sportsman bracket racers. IHRA's 2026 rules provide a path for eligible SuperSeries racers who win a Team Finals main-event class to qualify for the IHRA World Finals, subject to IHRA eligibility and validation requirements.

The event follows the Cap City $50K Bracket Race at the same track.

Dates, race-day schedules and entry details can change, so confirm the latest information before travelling.
