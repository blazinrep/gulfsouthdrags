What to expect

Baileyton Goodtime Dragstrip closed its 2026 season with the Mega Fest Season Finale on Saturday, November 21: motorcycle racing with $10,000 up for grabs. Classes included Street Kingz, R.W.Y.B (Run What You Brung), Street Gorillaz and Battle of the Ages. Winners, per the track's November 22 results post: Street Kingz — Tyree Givens (“Peezy”; runner-up “Two Face”); R.W.Y.B — Cody Spires (“bad company”); Street Gorillaz — “DoughBoy”. The Battle of the Ages winner's surname was not legible in the posted results and is not recorded here.
