What to expect

No Problem Raceway Park hosts “No Disrespect — Line Up” on Saturday, December 5, 2026. Gates open 10:00 AM, track hot at 12:00 noon, drivers meeting at 2:00 PM, one shakedown per class. Spectators $20, car and driver $60, cash only. Classes: Pops Peavy Full Size Truck Shootout ($150 buy-in, 70/30 split, track adds $500); Anything on 28s, $5,000 to win ($300 entry); Outlaw Street, $4,000 ($200 entry); 5.30 Index, $3,000 ($150 entry); Daily Driver, $3,000 ($150 entry).

Pre-event: Friday, December 4, a Happy Hour Practice Tree Race in the bar at 7:30 PM ($5 to enter, 400 pro tree). Adam Simoneaux will be selling nitrous bottles on property December 4th and 5th.
