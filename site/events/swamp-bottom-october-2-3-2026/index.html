Swamp Bottom October Race Weekend

Swamp Bottom Dragstrip — Columbia, MS
Friday 2 and Saturday 3 October 2026

Swamp Bottom Dragstrip returns October 2-3 with two nights of racing. Friday is TNT / Grudge night. Saturday features Street Cars racing for a $1,000 payout and All on 28s racing for a $2,500 payout. The track flyer also advertises a free tech card sponsored by T.L. Wallace Construction.
