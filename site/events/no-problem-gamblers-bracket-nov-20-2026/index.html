What to expect

No Problem Raceway Park wraps November with a Gamblers Race and a Saturday Bracket Race on November 21, 2026 — the last bracket race of the year. Friday Night TNT runs November 20. Entry $60 for Box, No Box and Bikes; a $1,200 payout was advertised on an earlier flyer, but the race it applies to is unconfirmed. Track prep by TL1 Traction Compound. Sunday November 22 appears on the event flyer, but no Sunday schedule, times, or payouts have been confirmed — details pending; confirm with the track before travelling.

No Problem's 19 November update (two flyers) confirms Friday gates 5:30 PM with the track hot at 6:30 PM and the Gamblers Race at ~8:30 PM, and Saturday gates 8:00 AM with the track hot at 9:00 AM (Juniors first, big cars at noon), with per-class Saturday purses listed in the schedule.
