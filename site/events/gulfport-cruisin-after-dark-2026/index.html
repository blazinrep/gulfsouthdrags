3rd Annual Cruisin' After Dark

Gulfport Dragway — Gulfport, MS
Thursday 8 October 2026

LATE-BREAKING ADDITION: Neil Esposito's Jersey Attitude Jet Dragster has been added to Gulfport Dragway's 3rd Annual Cruisin' After Dark on Thursday, Oct. 8. Gates open at 5 PM, the track is hot 6-11 PM, and spectator admission is $10.

What to expect

Cruisin' After Dark is part of Gulfport Dragway's Cruisin' the Coast week programming. Gulfport confirmed on Sept. 19 through its official Facebook account that Neil Esposito and the Jersey Attitude Jet Dragster are a late-breaking addition to the Thursday night show. The program also advertises a Wheel Standing Contest, a Smoke 'Em Till They Pop Contest, quarter-mile drag racing and a parade of cars.

The official Gulfport Dragway announcement lists spectator admission at $10. A current Gulfcoast Race Week event listing also says racing entry is an additional $20; confirm racer fees and contest participation requirements directly with the track before towing.

This event leads directly into the Southeast Gassers Association Gulf Coast Nationals at Gulfport on Oct. 9-10.
