What to expect

Footbrake classes are for cars without transbrakes or delay boxes — the driver launches off the footbrake, which puts more of the round on the driver and less on the electronics. A $5,000 purse is a significant draw for a Saturday bracket race.

Pro and Junior Dragster run the same day on a regular purse. Open track and test and tune runs the Thursday before, on 17 September.

Holiday Raceway publishes its dates as subject to change. Confirm with the track before travelling.
