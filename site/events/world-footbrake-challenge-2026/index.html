What to expect

Footbrake classes are for cars without transbrakes or delay boxes, launching off the footbrake, which puts more of the round on the driver.

It is the last event on Montgomery's published 2026 calendar, following the Gobble Wobble Thanksgiving Bracket from 20 to 22 November.
