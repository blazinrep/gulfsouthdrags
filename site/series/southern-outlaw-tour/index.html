Southern Outlaw Tour

Alabama and Mississippi

The Southern Outlaw Tour is a four-race drag racing series running at Montgomery International Dragway, Gulfport Dragway and Alabama International Dragway in Steele, with the finale back at Montgomery.

About this series

A four-race series announced for 2026, run in partnership between Montgomery International Dragway and DragCoverage. Montgomery's schedule names TJ Pruitt and DragCoverage as partners on the tour.

The rounds run at Montgomery International Dragway, Gulfport Dragway, Alabama International Dragway in Steele for its Night of Fire, and the finale back at Montgomery. Montgomery's published calendar shows finale qualifying with ProMods and 5.30, 6.00 and 7.00 index classes plus a $5,000 bracket race, with any tire size allowed, followed by the main event the next day.
