NHRA Summit Racing Equipment E.T. Series

National, South Central Division 4 locally

The NHRA Summit Racing Equipment E.T. Series presented by Hoosier is NHRA's bracket racing programme for member tracks. Swamp Bottom Dragstrip is eligible to run it as a new South Central Division member track.

Schedule not confirmed

We have not been able to verify this series’ current schedule or full list of host tracks. If you run or race it, send us the schedule and we will publish it.

About this series

Member tracks run weekly and points events under the series banner, with champions advancing to divisional and national competition. NHRA's South Central Division covers Mississippi, Louisiana, Arkansas, Oklahoma and Texas.

Swamp Bottom joined the Member Track Network in July 2026, which also makes it eligible for the Summit Jr. Drag Racing League, NHRA Street Legal, Jr. Street and specialty events such as Summit King of the Track. Its first season schedule has not been published yet.
