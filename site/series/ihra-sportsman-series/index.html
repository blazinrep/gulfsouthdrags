IHRA Sportsman Series

National, with Gulf Coast member tracks

The IHRA Sportsman Series is the International Hot Rod Association's bracket racing programme for member tracks. Gulfport Dragway runs an IHRA Track Championship Series, and Hub City Dragway has historically run IHRA ET bracket racing.

About this series

IHRA member tracks run their own points programmes under the series banner, with track champions advancing to larger events. Gulfport's version includes the Summer Iron Man Challenge.

Check with the individual track for the current points schedule — these change from season to season and are not published centrally.

Where does IHRA Sportsman Series race?

It races at Hub City Dragway in Hattiesburg, MS, Gulfport Dragway in Gulfport, MS.

Who sanctions IHRA Sportsman Series?

IHRA.
