About this track

A quarter-mile track in Steele, in northeast Alabama off Interstate 59 at exit 174. It opened in 1994.

By social following it is the largest track in this directory, with around 49,000 followers and an 88 percent recommendation rate across more than 1,700 reviews. It posts regularly and was confirmed active on 12 September 2026.

The signature programme is Moonlight Madness, run every Friday night from 9pm to 1am. That late slot is unusual and worth planning around if you are travelling.

This is the farthest track currently listed here, roughly 245 miles from Hattiesburg. It is included because it draws from a wide area and because racers from north Mississippi make the trip.
