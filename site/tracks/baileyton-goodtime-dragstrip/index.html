About this track

An eighth-mile track in Baileyton, north Alabama, known locally as the Goodtime. Confirmed active on 12 September 2026, with a 96 percent recommendation rate across 381 reviews.

The thing that sets Baileyton apart is that it guarantees its purses regardless of car count. Most tracks post payouts as based on a minimum number of entries, so a thin turnout means a smaller cheque. Baileyton pays what it advertises either way, in both classes.

As advertised for the September 2026 Saturday programme, Footbrake paid $5,000 to win with $500 to the runner-up, $100 to the semis and $50 to the quarter-finals, on a $100 single or $160 double entry with a $50 buyback. Pro paid $1,000 to win with $300 runner-up on a $60 entry and $40 buyback. Jr Dragster paid $300 to win with $50 runner-up on a $40 entry and $20 buyback.

Gates open at 4pm with time runs from 6pm. Spectators $10, children 12 and under free.

The track is listed on the WDRA member track list as Baileyton Drag Strip. Schedule announcements go out through Facebook.
