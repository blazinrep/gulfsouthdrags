About this track

A multi-discipline motorsports facility in Belle Rose, in the Louisiana river parishes between Baton Rouge and Houma. Confirmed active on 12 September 2026. The track is running its 25th anniversary season, having opened in 2001.

The drag strip is a quarter-mile sanctioned by NHRA. Alongside it sits a 1.8-mile asphalt road course and more than 200 acres used for concerts, car shows and festivals. That mix is unusual in this region — most tracks listed here do one thing.

The regular programme runs TNT Test ’n Tune on selected Friday nights and Bracket Masters on selected Saturdays, with road course events, Super Saturdays run with Flip Flop Promotions, and private pro test sessions bookable by phone. The track publishes a calendar of events each month on its Facebook page.

It also hosts the Pure Speed Drag Racing Experience, a ride-and-drive programme for people who want to run a race car without owning one, booked separately from the racing calendar.

Two phone numbers are in use: 225-445-3498 and 985-369-3692. The number printed on the track's own website has an extra digit and cannot be dialled as written.
