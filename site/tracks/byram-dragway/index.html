About this track

A quarter-mile asphalt track on Siwell Road in Byram, immediately south of Jackson, and the closest strip to the Jackson metro.

It was formerly known as Jackson Dragway and now operates as Byram Dragway. The change is why the track is easy to write off as dead: directory listings under the old name still carry a phone number that is disconnected, Google Maps reviews under the old name stop around 2024, and no current schedule appears under either name outside Facebook. The track itself is running weekly.

The regular programme is Tuesday Night Midnight Madness, billed as test and tune with grudge racing. Gate prices as advertised in September 2026 are $25 for racers and $15 for spectators. The track describes itself as the little track with all the action.

Schedule and event announcements go out through the Facebook page rather than a website, so check there before travelling.
