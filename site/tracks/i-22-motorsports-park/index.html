About this track

An eighth-mile track in Eldridge, northwest Alabama. Confirmed active on 12 September 2026, with a 98 percent recommendation rate on its social page.

The track runs Super Pro, Footbrake and Jr Dragster, and puts up serious money for a track its size. Its Labor Day Weekend Bracket Bash in September 2026 paid $7,500 to win in Super Pro on the Saturday, $5,000 on the Sunday and $2,000 on the Friday, with entries of $175, $100 and $50 respectively and buybacks available. Footbrake paid $2,500, $1,500 and $1,000 across the same three days on entries of $80, $75 and $50. Payouts are posted as based on car counts — 75 cars for the Saturday Super Pro purse, 50 for the Sunday.

Weekend passes were offered at $250 single and $380 double for Super Pro, and $140 single and $250 double for Footbrake. Friday gates opened at 3pm with open time runs from 5pm to 7pm and racing to follow.

The track also runs invitational shootouts. Schedule announcements go out through Facebook rather than a website, so check there before travelling.
