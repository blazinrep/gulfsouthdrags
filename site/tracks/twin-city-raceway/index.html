About this track

An IHRA member track in Monroe, north Louisiana, running under the House of Hook banner. Confirmed active on 12 September 2026.

The bracket programme runs four classes. As advertised for the September 2026 race, Super Pro pays $5,000 to win with a $125 entry, No Box pays $2,000 with a $90 entry, Street pays $500 with a $40 entry, and Jr Dragster pays $500 with a $40 entry. Runner-up, semi-final and quarter-final money is posted for each class, and buybacks are available. Car counts are capped by class.

Gates typically open at noon with time trials from 1pm. Spectators $15, children $10. TNT sessions run $50 with a diaper required and a TNT stop after the third round of eliminations.

The track also hosts a Gasser Revival. Google lists this track as Twin City Motorsports Park at 3695 Prairie Rd with the number 318-557-3284, while the track's own Facebook page uses Twin City Raceway, gives the address as 3695 Hwy 841 and the number 318-266-7441. Prairie Rd and Highway 841 meet at the property, so both are correct. Use either name when searching.

Schedule announcements go out through Facebook rather than a website, so check there before travelling.
