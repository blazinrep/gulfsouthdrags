About this track

An eighth-mile concrete track at 1301 Racetrack Rd in Atmore, just off I-65 near the Alabama and Florida line, and for years the nearest Alabama option to the Mississippi Gulf Coast.

It is permanently closed. Google lists the business as permanently closed as of 12 September 2026. The listing still carries a 4.5 star rating from 355 reviews and photographs of a packed house, which gives some idea of what the place was when it was running.

It was still listed as a current member track by a national sanctioning body when we compiled this directory, which is worth knowing if you are relying on those lists to plan a weekend. Member track lists record who joined, not who is still racing.

If you are looking for an eighth-mile in that part of Alabama, tell us and we will go find one.
