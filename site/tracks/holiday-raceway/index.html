About this track

A drag strip on Highway 11 in Woodstock, between Birmingham and Tuscaloosa, and one of the few tracks in this directory that publishes a full season schedule rather than announcing races week to week.

The regular pattern is open track and test and tune on Thursdays, with bracket racing on scheduled Saturdays running Pro, Footbrake and Junior Dragster. Several Saturdays carry raised purses: $5,000 to win in Pro or Footbrake, and $1,000 in Junior Dragster. A number of those count towards IHRA points.

The season runs late March through November and mixes bracket racing with bigger draws. The 2026 calendar included the Southeast Gassers Association over two days in May, a Streetcar Challenge in June, Street Outlaws small tire shootouts with Ryan Martin and Murder Nova across two days in August, King of the Beach in October, a 17th annual cruise-in with car, bike and truck shows in April, the Woodstock Music Festival in May, and a Thanksgiving Throwdown sound and audio competition in November.

The track closes for Memorial Day, Independence Day and Labor Day weekends. All dates are published as subject to change, so confirm before travelling.
